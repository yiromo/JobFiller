          Generate a tailored CV
          
    
          
      Rewrites one of your CVs for a specific position and saves the result as a new PDF you can
      pick when filling a form. Employers, titles, dates, degrees and numbers are copied from the
      source CV and never invented. Your stack gets reordered and renamed to its current 2026
      naming for the target role. A technology is only added when you explicitly ask for it below —
      asking to prioritize or lead with something reorders what is already on your CV, it never
      adds it. Anything added, and any wording that is not in the source CV, is listed back to you
      so you can check it before sending the CV anywhere. Employment titles, employers and dates are
      copied from the source CV; a line of the form Title Dreams Group as Full-Stack
      Engineer in the box below is the only way to change a title, and each one you override
      is listed back to you.

              Settings

      guessed from your CV or the job posting — that's a hard rule. Add your own answer to each
      one here and the AI will use it to pick or word the answer for each site's actual field
