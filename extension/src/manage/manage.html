    Page access

    Some application forms (Newton/gnewton and similar ATS pages) load the actual form inside an embedded iframe from a different domain than the page you're on. Scanning those needs permission to read that domain too — grant it once here rather than per-site.

    Settings

    EEO/demographic questions (gender, race, veteran, disability status, etc.) are never auto-filled by any AI or heuristic — that's a hard rule. If you'd rather not retype your own answers on every application, add them here and the extension will fill them exactly as typed, nothing inferred. Leave a row blank to keep that question skipped.
